About the company

A specialist contractor for other construction systems.

SEAREFICO CORPORATION is registered for the installation of other construction systems. We bring together the trades that make a building function: movement, power, climate, safety, communication, and energy.

From elevators and automatic doors to industrial refrigeration, fire protection, and medical gas, our work is planned around code, continuity of operation, and a clean finish on occupied sites.

  • Single-source coordination across related building systems
  • Electrical construction works up to 35kV
  • Industrial, commercial, and healthcare-ready utilities
